I played telephone with my website.

Round one got everything: exact hex codes, the font name, the wordmark. After that, one AI described each post, and another made the next post from that description alone. Five rounds, twice.

What I noticed

Even on the first try, with my exact color codes and font name in the prompt, the AI changed my soft charcoal text to plain black and swapped my font for a generic sans. The yellow came closest, but it shifted from round to round.

The font was never right. Not once in ten rounds did it look like Bricolage Grotesque. When the AI described my posts, it named Bricolage once as a guess and otherwise reached for Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Sans, the fonts most AI posts already use.

The two runs failed in different ways. One kept my layout but drifted on color. The other chased the color and lost the layout, even putting things in the wrong order. Its color scores improved while the post itself looked worse, which is a good reminder that a number only measures what you point it at.

How it was made

Images by gpt-image-1, descriptions by claude-sonnet-5-5. For each post I pulled the colors that cover at least half a percent of the image and matched each real color to its closest one, using CIEDE2000 for ΔE. Fonts are as named by the describing model, so treat them as what it heard, not ground truth. Two runs is a small sample; this is a quick experiment, not a study.
